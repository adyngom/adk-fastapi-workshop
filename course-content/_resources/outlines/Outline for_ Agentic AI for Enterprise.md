# Outline for: Agentic AI for Enterprise

This cohort is a hands-on course, and it is highly recommended that you first complete the AI Solution Architect course offered by ELVTR. While some programming skills are required, familiarity with Jupyter Notebook and Python is strongly advised. By the end of the course, you will have developed two Agentic applications, of your choosing, from start to finish, suitable for enterprise-level deployment.

This course addresses a common failure faced by the majority of enterprise companies regarding Generative AI and Agentic AI. According to the **MIT NANDA** report titled *“The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025,”* an *astonishing **95%** of pilot projects for generative AI in enterprises fail* to produce measurable results, while only 5% achieve a significant increase in revenue. The report emphasizes that the key issue is not the performance of the AI models themselves. Still, companies face the challenge of effectively integrating generative AI into their everyday processes. It highlights what the report terms a “learning gap” that needs to be addressed.

In the first ten lessons, the objectives are to help you select an appropriate application, review potential biases or ethical issues, review advanced Agentic architecture, write a practical Agentic application, and utilize AI to assist an Agent or create an Agentic AI. Additionally, you will learn how to ensure the quality of the application, deploy it with either a complete or minimal user interface, scale it, gather feedback, and upgrade or maintain the Agentic application from an enterprise perspective.

In the last six lessons, you will repeat the process for another, more complex Agentic application of your choice.

## Lesson 4: Advanced Agentic Architecture

1. Define the difference between Agentic Architecture and Agentic Framework

   1. Architecture \= the blueprint of how the house is structured.

   2. Framework \= the set of tools (wood, beams, concrete, scaffolding) you use to construct it.

2. Single-Agent Architectures

   1. Core Advantages: Simplicity, low overhead, suitable for bounded tasks.

3. Multi-Agent Architectures (Vertical, Horizontal, Hybrid)

   1. Core Advantages: Modularity, scalability, task specialization

4. Agentic Mesh or Platform-Based Agentic Architectures

   1. Core Advantages: Future-proofing, resilience, vendor-neutrality

5. Agentic AI Frameworks & Orchestration Ecosystems

   1. Core Advantages: Rapid development, oversight, and coordination.
