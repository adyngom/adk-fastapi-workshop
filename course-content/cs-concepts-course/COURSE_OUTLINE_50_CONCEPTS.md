## 📋 THE 50 CONCEPTS

### **PART 1: When Your Prompts Get Expensive (Concepts 1-10)**

*The moment: "I'm spending $50/month on ChatGPT for simple formatting"*

**Concept 1: Pattern Matching (Regular Expressions)**
- **The Story:** Your 3-hour YouTube transcript formatting disaster
- **The Solution:** Regex finds `[00:15:42]` → converts to markdown headers
- **Cost Comparison:** $3.75 (ChatGPT) vs $0 (regex)
- **When to use:** Predictable repeated patterns
- **Exercise:** Extract all email addresses from text
- **Duration:** 8 min video

**Concept 2: Find and Replace Patterns**
- **The Story:** Updating 200 customer emails with personalization
- **The Solution:** Find `{name}` → replace with actual names
- **When to use:** Exact replacements across multiple items
- **Exercise:** Template-based email generation
- **Duration:** 6 min video

**Concept 3: String Manipulation Basics**
- **The Story:** Cleaning up messy CSV exports
- **The Solution:** trim(), toLowerCase(), split(), join()
- **When to use:** Consistent text transformations
- **Exercise:** Clean up phone numbers format
- **Duration:** 7 min video

**Concept 4: CSV/JSON Parsing**
- **The Story:** "ChatGPT, convert this CSV to JSON" ($5 in tokens)
- **The Solution:** Papa Parse or built-in JSON.parse()
- **When to use:** Structured data transformations
- **Exercise:** CSV → JSON → filtered results
- **Duration:** 8 min video

**Concept 5: File Operations (Read/Write)**
- **The Story:** Manually copying data between files
- **The Solution:** fs.readFile(), fs.writeFile()
- **When to use:** Batch file processing
- **Exercise:** Read 10 files, combine into one
- **Duration:** 7 min video

**Concept 6: Basic Loops vs LLM Iteration**
- **The Story:** "Process these 100 items" → 100 ChatGPT calls
- **The Solution:** for loop processes all at once
- **Cost:** $50 (100 ChatGPT calls) vs $0 (one script)
- **Exercise:** Apply discount to 50 products
- **Duration:** 6 min video

**Concept 7: Conditional Logic (If/Else)**
- **The Story:** "ChatGPT, if price > 100, apply discount"
- **The Solution:** Simple if statement
- **When to use:** Rule-based decisions
- **Exercise:** Price calculator with tiers
- **Duration:** 6 min video

**Concept 8: Data Validation**
- **The Story:** Bad data getting into your system
- **The Solution:** Validation rules before processing
- **When to use:** Form submissions, data imports
- **Exercise:** Email validator
- **Duration:** 7 min video

**Concept 9: Template Systems**
- **The Story:** Generating 500 similar documents
- **The Solution:** Handlebars, Jinja, or template literals
- **When to use:** Consistent formatting with variable data
- **Exercise:** Email template with personalization
- **Duration:** 8 min video

**Concept 10: When Exact Beats Approximate**
- **The Summary Concept:** Decision framework
- **Checklist:** When to code vs when to prompt
- **When to use:** Before opening ChatGPT!
- **Exercise:** Decision tree - is this a code or AI task?
- **Duration:** 10 min video

**Part 1 Total:** ~75 minutes video content + 10 written posts
